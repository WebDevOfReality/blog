# Welcome to WebDev of Reality

## The project

**Spider-Verse OS** is a distributed operating system that turns my homelab into
a unified pool of compute, storage, and intelligence. Every device in my house —
servers, media boxes, phones — becomes a node. One computer made of many.

The pieces:

- **Earths** — physical devices, named after Marvel multiverse universes
- **The Web of Reality** — a Nebula mesh network connecting them all
- **Spiders** — AI agents that deploy, sync, and heal workloads on demand
- **The AI Harness** — natural language in, Spider dispatch out

It's ambitious and probably a little unhinged. That's the point.

## Why "WebDev of Reality"

It's a triple pun. **Web** — the mesh network. **Dev** — the engineering lens.
**Of Reality** — grounded in real hardware, real costs, real 2 AM failures. Not
theory. Not slides. Actual machines making actual noise in my basement.

## The part I'm proudest of

This blog has an **AI policy**, and it's not a suggestion — it's enforced in the
build. Every post carries a visible provenance label:

- **Human written** — no AI involved
- **AI assisted** — AI helped draft or edit; the model is named
- **Generated with AI from human source** — like a vlog transcript reworked into
  a post; my words, my ideas, AI as the adapter

If a post tried to ship with AI involvement and no signed disclosure, the build
would fail. And there's one absolute rule: **no AI-generated art, ever.** The
logo you'll see here soon is being drawn by a human graphic designer, because
creative work deserves a human.

The ideas here are mine. Some of the formatting help wasn't — and now you know
exactly when that's the case.
