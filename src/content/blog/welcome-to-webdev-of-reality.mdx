That is what Adrian Toomes says to the Tinkerer in Spider-Man: Homecoming before he decides to become The Vulture. That quote has more weight to this whole WebDev of Reality than I first realized.

1. **Spider-Man is my favorite Hero —** have been a comic book nerd since watching the 90s Spider-Man cartoon. Of course I would reach for a Spidey quote first. That quote by Toomes feels very relevant today in the fast changing world of AI.
2. **I love to Tinker —** fitting Phineas T. Mason A.K.A the Tinkerer would be in this scene. I have always been a tinkerer. I remember taking apart electronics and trying to build new things. When I was 9 I took a Sony Walkman, with a broken headphone jack, apart. Then, stripped the wire of a speaker from a “boombox” and soldiered it to the Walkman. I had myself my own portable speaker before Bluetooth was a thing!
3. **Spider-verse OS —** for me to truly understand something I need to break it down to the smallest possible unit, then rebuild it back. To truly understand computers I am building my own Operating System called Spider-Verse OS. More on this in a future post!

### “Who the hell is this guy, and what makes him an expert?!”

Short answer is **I’m not!**  Definitely not an expert but I have been building software for over a decade. My name is Anthony Renteria, I am a Senior Software Engineer at [Olo](https://www.olo.com/). Olo has been (what my computer science professor, Dr. Beard would call) a trial-by-fire 🔥. Olo provides restaurants the ability to provide their customers with online ordering. Before DoorDash, Ubereats and even the iPhone, there was [text-based mobile ordering provided my Olo!](https://fortune.com/2026/09/01/online-restaurant-ordering-ecommerce-marketplaces/)
